Here's what you need to know

Yes! Tart cherry juice is naturally rich in melatonin and anthocyanins, which work together to regulate sleep cycles and ease muscle recovery. Drinking about 8 ounces (240ml) of unsweetened Montmorency tart cherry juice about 1 to 2 hours before bedtime has been shown to improve both sleep duration and sleep quality.
